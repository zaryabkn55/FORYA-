VISUAL SYSTEM
  Colour      Charcoal #1A1A1A on white #FFFFFF. Greys (#666666, #E3E3E3) only for secondary
              text and hairlines. No accent colour.

8. BEFORE YOU POST — one thing to check
--------------------------------------
The editorial photographs (posts 03, 04, 07 and the reel cover) come from the app's own image
set, which says it was made for FORya with no third-party rights. But the same file describes
them in two incompatible ways: once as generated images, once as a shoot with real models.
  - If they were generated: add Instagram's AI label when posting, and never caption them as
    customers or real people's outfits.
  - If they were a real shoot: make sure the model releases cover social media and advertising.
Either is fine. Just know which it is before they go out under the FORya name.
