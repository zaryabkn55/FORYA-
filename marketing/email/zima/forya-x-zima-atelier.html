When the forecast turns cold,
we’d like the coat to be ZIMA.

 

A short preview of ZIMA Atelier on FORya, built from the pieces in your current collection.

 
 

Dear Kristin and Daniela,

I’m Zaryab, co-founder of FORya, an outfit planner built in London. Our users tell FORya about something they’re going to (a dinner in Mayfair, a wedding, afternoon tea), and it builds the whole look for that day, starting with the clothes they already own and the forecast for that place and hour.

When a Moment turns cold and the wardrobe has nothing right to wear over the look, FORya suggests the piece that’s missing. Through the winter, that piece is almost always a coat. Pieces like your pink tweed with mink sleeves, or the light pink coat with the chinchilla collar, are exactly what we’d want our users to find there.

So we built a preview of how ZIMA would appear on FORya: twelve pieces from your current collection, a boutique page of your own, and the Moments your coats would be suggested for.

Why FORya for ZIMA

Made for the way ZIMA already works.

Your coats, when they’re needed
FORya adds an outer layer when the forecast for a Moment calls for one. From November to March, that’s most looks.
Built for limited editions
We only show what’s available. When a fabric runs out and a coat leaves your collection, it leaves FORya too.
Your clients, your conversation
FORya never takes payment. Enquiries come to you by WhatsApp, DM or a showroom appointment, exactly as they do today.
A boutique, not a listing
Your campaign, collections, archive and fittings in one place, presented the way ZIMA presents itself.
