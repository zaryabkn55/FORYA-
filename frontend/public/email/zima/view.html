A short preview of ZIMA Atelier on FORya, built from the pieces in your current collection.

Dear Kristin and Daniela,

I’m Zaryab, co-founder of FORya, an outfit planner built in London. Our users tell FORya about something they’re going to (a dinner in Mayfair, a wedding, afternoon tea), and it builds the whole look for that day, starting with the clothes they already own and the forecast for that place and hour.

When a Moment turns cold and the wardrobe has nothing right to wear over the look, FORya suggests the piece that’s missing. Through the winter, that piece is almost always a coat. Pieces like your pink tweed with mink sleeves, or the light pink coat with the chinchilla collar, are exactly what we’d want our users to find there.

So we built a preview of how ZIMA would appear on FORya. Scroll through it below.

The simulation

ZIMA on FORya, in four screens.

01 · The Moment

A plan, not a feed.

She tells FORya about afternoon tea at The Berkeley on Saturday at 3pm. FORya reads the forecast for Knightsbridge at that hour (6°, clear) and her dress code, then explains why the look needs a coat before suggesting one.

02 · The look

Styled with what she owns.

Her own slip dress, boots and bag come first. Your Pink Tweed Swarovski Crystal Coat completes the look, and Look Check confirms it’s smart, complete and warm enough.

03 · The piece

Sold your way.

Shown as a limited edition, price on request, exactly as you sell today. Enquiries go straight to your WhatsApp or to a fitting at Bagista in Knightsbridge. FORya never takes payment.

04 · Your page

A home for the collection.

ZIMA gets its own page in Brands with your story, your categories and only the pieces still available. When a fabric runs out, the coat leaves FORya too.

Why FORya for ZIMA
